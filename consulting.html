The Problem

The honest truth about your team.

You're not wrong for being frustrated. But your team isn't getting the feedback they need from someone who isn't afraid to give it.

Expectations live in your head.

You know what excellence looks like. But you've never translated that into language your EA, PA, or Chief of Staff can actually execute against. They're guessing — and you're frustrated when they guess wrong.

Internal HR can't give honest feedback.

Your people will never tell you the full truth. Neither will the HR department that reports to you. You need an outsider with no political agenda — someone whose only job is to make your operation better.

You've outgrown your team's capability.

Your business evolved. Your standards raised. But your team's skills stayed the same. Nobody invested in closing that gap — so now you're doing the work of three people because it's faster than explaining it again.

Annual reviews are fiction.

Once-a-year check-ins are performance theater. By the time you give feedback, the damage is done. Real performance management happens daily, weekly, monthly — not in a December slide deck.

The Process

How we work.

We don't parachute in with a binder. We embed into your culture, learn what's actually happening, and build a system that stays after we leave.

01

Embed

We immerse ourselves in your operation. Shadow your team, understand the culture, learn how decisions get made, and identify the gap between what you expect and what's being delivered.

02

Discover

Confidential interviews with current and former employees. We hear what nobody says to your face. We build a true 360-degree view — not the sanitized version HR puts on a slide.

03

Deep Dive

We present our findings — the uncomfortable ones included. Where expectations aren't communicated. Where talent is misaligned. Where culture says one thing but behavior says another.

04

Manage

Action plans, performance frameworks, training programs, and ongoing management. In some cases, we hire and fire on your behalf. We stay as long as it takes to make the change permanent.

Assessment Cadence

Performance is a rhythm, not an event.

Daily

Pulse Checks

Quick alignment touchpoints. Are priorities clear? Are blockers being flagged? We catch drift before it becomes a problem.

Weekly

Performance Reviews

Structured 1-on-1s that focus on growth, not grievances. What's working, what's not, and what needs to change this week — not next quarter.

Monthly

Deep Assessments

Comprehensive evaluations against expectations. We measure, document, and present findings with zero spin. You get the truth.

Annual

Strategic Reviews

Full organizational health review. Team composition, role alignment, compensation benchmarking, succession planning. The big picture — supported by a year of data.
