Tools / Balanced Life Budget

A budget for a balanced life.

Not a spreadsheet. Not a lecture. A tool that helps you see where your money goes — and where it should go — across what you need, what you want, and what you're building for the future.

Start building

Built by Jeremiah Gutierrez

01 / Your Profile

Who is this for?

This tool is for anyone who's tired of wondering where the money went. Whether you earn $3,000 a month or $30,000, the framework is the same: Needs keep the lights on, Wants make life worth living, and Future is what you're building.

Most people budget for bills and forget to budget for life. That's not balance — that's just accounting. Fill in the profile to personalize your export, then move to the budget below.

Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. Everything stays in your browser.

Your budget profile

05 / Analysis

Savings advice

Enter your numbers above, then click the button below. The analysis engine examines your spending patterns, compares them to benchmarks, and generates specific, actionable advice on how to save.

Budget analysis

Enter your income and expenses, then click Analyze my budget for personalized savings advice.

Balance means budgeting for life — not just for bills.

The 50/30/20 split (needs/wants/future) is a starting guideline, not a rule — your right mix depends on your income, debt load, and life stage. Nothing you enter here is saved or transmitted. If you refresh, it resets to blank. Use Print/Save PDF or Export Excel to keep a copy. This is a budgeting framework, not financial advice.
